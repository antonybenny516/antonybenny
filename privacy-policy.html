Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Antony Benny, trading as Bamboost LLP ("I", "me", "my"), collects, uses, and protects information when you visit antonybennyseo.in (the "Site") or use the contact form on it.

Information I Collect

When you submit the contact form on this Site, I collect the information you provide: your name, email address, an optional website URL, and your message. This information is sent directly to a Google Forms backend for me to review and respond to your enquiry.

I do not collect payment information, and the Site does not require you to create an account.

Analytics & Cookies

This Site uses Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager to understand how visitors use the Site (for example, which pages are viewed and how visitors arrived). These tools may set cookies and collect information such as your approximate location, browser type, and pages visited. This data is aggregated and used only to improve the Site's content and performance.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, or opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

How I Use Your Information

  • To respond to enquiries submitted through the contact form.
  • To understand and improve how the Site is used, via aggregated analytics.
  • To meet legal obligations, where applicable.

Third-Party Services

This Site relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy: Google Forms (contact form submissions), Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager (site analytics), Google Fonts (web fonts), and jsDelivr (content delivery for Bootstrap). I do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained only as long as needed to respond to your enquiry and for reasonable record-keeping. You may request deletion of your submitted information at any time by emailing me at the address below.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information you've submitted through this Site by contacting me directly.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the Site or applicable law. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.
