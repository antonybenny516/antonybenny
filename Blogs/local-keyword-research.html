How to Do Local Keyword Research for SEO

Antony Benny March 30, 2026 5 min read

If you run a local business, showing up on Google when nearby customers search for your services is everything. But here's the truth - generic SEO won't cut it. You need a local keyword research strategy built specifically for your city, region, or neighbourhood.

This guide walks you through exactly how to do that.

What Is Local Keyword Research?

Local keyword research is the process of finding search terms that people in a specific geographic area use to find products or services near them.

Instead of targeting broad terms like "digital marketing agency", you focus on phrases like "digital marketing agency in thrissur" or "SEO services near me".

These location-specific keywords carry strong buying intent - meaning the person searching is usually ready to take action.

Why Local Keywords Matter More Than You Think

A user searching "best SEO consultant in Kerala" is far more likely to convert into a paying client than someone who searches "SEO consultant".

The geographic modifier filters out casual visitors and brings in people who are actively looking for services in their area.

This is why local keyword research sits at the heart of every successful local SEO campaign.

Step 1: Start With Your Core Services

Begin by listing your primary services or products:

  • What do you offer?
  • Who do you serve?
  • Where do you serve them?

Example: If you're a web designer in Kerala, your core terms might be web design, website development, and WordPress developer.

Step 2: Add Location Modifiers

Attach geographic modifiers to each core term:

  • City: web designer in Calicut
  • Region: web designer in Malabar
  • State: web designer in Kerala
  • Near me searches
  • Landmarks: web designer near Calicut Beach

Also consider variations like Calicut and thrissur, as users may search both.

Step 3: Use the Right Tools

You don’t need expensive tools. Use:

  • Google Autocomplete – Real user search suggestions
  • Google Keyword Planner – Location-based data
  • Google Search Console – Existing traffic insights
  • Ubersuggest / Ahrefs – Advanced keyword analysis

For deeper learning, refer to trusted resources like Moz’s Beginner’s Guide to SEO.

Step 4: Analyse Search Intent

Understand what users want:

  • Informational: Write blog posts
  • Navigational: Optimize contact pages
  • Transactional: Optimize service pages

Matching content to intent is key to ranking success.

Step 5: Check Your Competition

Search your keywords and analyze:

  • Are competitors local or national?
  • Is content detailed or thin?
  • Is Google Map Pack showing?

If small local businesses rank, you have a strong chance to compete.

Step 6: Map Keywords to Pages

Assign keywords to specific pages:

Keyword Target Page
SEO expert in Kerala Homepage
Local SEO services thrissur Services Page
How to do local keyword research Blog Post
Contact SEO consultant Kerala Contact Page

Avoid targeting the same keyword on multiple pages to prevent keyword cannibalization.

Final Thoughts

Local keyword research is not a one-time task. Search behavior changes, competitors evolve, and algorithms update.

Revisit your keyword strategy regularly to stay ahead. The right keywords bring the right customers.
